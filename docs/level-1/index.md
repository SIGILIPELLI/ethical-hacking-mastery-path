# Level 1 · Foundations & Legal Lab

Before anyone runs a single scan, two things have to be true: the work must be **authorized**, and
there must be a **safe place to practise**. Level 1 settles both, then teaches the opening moves of
every engagement — reconnaissance, scanning and enumeration — against targets you build and own.

You will finish this level able to legally stand up an isolated lab, understand the lifecycle a
professional test follows, drive the Linux command line a tester relies on, and map a target host
from "I know its IP" to "I have a ranked list of its services and likely weak points."

## Modules

1. **[What Ethical Hacking Is — Authorization, Scope & the Law](01-what-ethical-hacking-is.md)** — the
   difference permission makes, scope, rules of engagement, and the laws you must know.
2. **[Building a Legal, Isolated Practice Lab](02-building-a-lab.md)** — host-only networking,
   intentionally vulnerable targets, and keeping the lab off the internet.
3. **[The Penetration Testing Lifecycle](03-pentest-lifecycle.md)** — PTES and the OSSTMM, and the
   phases from pre-engagement to reporting.
4. **[The Linux Command Line You'll Actually Use](04-linux-command-line.md)** — the commands,
   pipes and text tools that carry a test.
5. **[Networking Through a Tester's Lens](05-networking-for-testers.md)** — TCP/IP, ports, the
   handshake, and what each layer gives an attacker.
6. **[Passive Reconnaissance & OSINT](06-passive-recon-osint.md)** — gathering information without
   touching the target, and the scope rules that still apply.
7. **[Active Scanning with Nmap](07-active-scanning-nmap.md)** — host discovery, port states, scan
   types, timing and NSE, run against your own lab.
8. **[Service Enumeration](08-service-enumeration.md)** — turning open ports into named, versioned
   services and a map of the attack surface.
9. **[Vulnerabilities, CVE & CVSS](09-vulnerabilities-cve-cvss.md)** — how weaknesses are named,
   scored and prioritised.
10. **[Project — Recon & Enumeration of Your Lab Range](10-project-recon-enumeration.md)** — a full
    discovery pass written up as a findings worksheet.
